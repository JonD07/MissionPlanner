The data velocity tests are meant to find the relationship between distance and data (Q) size.
Rather than the inverse square law of electromagnetism, this distance-packet size relationship
may be more useful.

Q will be varied from 0.001 (1kB) to 16.384 (16MB), a difference of (2^16), and graphed against distance.

Velocity will then be varied with the expectation that it will move the (Sigmoid) graph
described above left and right across the distance axis.
